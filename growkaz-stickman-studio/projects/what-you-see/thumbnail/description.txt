Be Wary of Your Eyes: The Lore Behind What You See
How What You See Rewires You (From the Evil Eye to Your Feed)
A Picture of Eyes Made People Pay 3x More. Your Screen Is Doing Worse.

DESCRIPTION
In 2006, a photocopy of two eyes taped above an office honesty box made people pay nearly three times as much. Nobody was watching. It was just paper.

So what is your screen doing to you?

This is a case file on the strange power of what we see: from the evil eye and Medusa, to a hypnotist debunked by Benjamin Franklin, a writer who nearly collapsed in front of a fresco, the PR stunt that sold cigarettes as freedom, an ad line that turned diamonds into a tradition, a fake subliminal-advertising study that panicked a nation, the Bobo doll, "mean world syndrome," and the island where television changed how girls saw their own bodies in three years.

Then it comes home: infinite scroll, a secret mood experiment on almost 700,000 people, and filters that turned the norm into you, edited.

From the screen... to the norm. Be wary of your eyes.
