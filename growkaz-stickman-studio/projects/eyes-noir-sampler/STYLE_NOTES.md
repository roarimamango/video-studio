# "The Lore Behind What You See": style notes (approved noir direction)

- Format: long-form 16:9, not Shorts.
- Look: `noir` style (ink blacks, slate, amber lamps and streetlights, one blood-red accent, heavy grain and film scratches).
- 2D on 3D: paper cut-out silhouettes with cream edges inside lit 3D sets; case-file graphics (typewriter strips, evidence tags, stamps, clippings).
- Camera: calm "rabbit hole" moves. Glide, then dive into an object (pupil, painting, screen) and iris out of it into the next scene. No chaos camera.
- PACING (user feedback): the sampler (about 3.4 s shots, about 0.9 s dives) felt a bit too fast. For the full video:
  - median shot about 4.5–6 s, with fewer hard cuts
  - dives about 1.3–1.6 s, iris transitions about 0.6–0.8 s
  - labels type slower (about 18 cps) and stay on screen longer
  - keep the rabbit-hole dive for chapter and exhibit changes; within an exhibit use slow pushes and dissolves
