| Exhibit | Claim in the script | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | Eyes above the honesty box led to nearly 3× the payments | Bateson, Nettle & Roberts (2006), *Biology Letters* 2(3):412–414. The paper says "nearly three times"; the script doesn't use 2.76. https://www.staff.ncl.ac.uk/melissa.bateson/Bateson_etal_2006.pdf |
| A | Evil-eye belief across ancient cultures; Medusa | General history. The script says "thousands of years" rather than a specific date. |
| B | Mesmer; 1784 royal commission with Franklin; blindfold tests | Well documented (Franklin and Lavoisier commission, 1784). |
| C | Stendhal at Santa Croce, 1817; named by psychiatrist Graziella Magherini (Florence, 1979) | Stendhal, *Rome, Naples and Florence* (1817). |
| D | Torches of Freedom, Easter Parade, March 31, 1929, for American Tobacco | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torches_of_Freedom. Head counts vary (about 10 to 12), so the script says "a handful". |
| E | "A Diamond Is Forever", Frances Gerety, N.W. Ayer, 1947 | https://www.startribune.com/how-an-ad-slogan-brought-diamonds-to-the-masses/244743061/. The script avoids the weakly sourced 10%→80% figures. |
| F | Vicary, Fort Lee, 1957; "Eat popcorn / Drink Coca-Cola"; popcorn up more than 50%; called it a gimmick in 1962 | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Vicary · https://www.aaaa.org/great-subliminal-advertising-scare/ |
| G | Bandura's Bobo doll study, Stanford, 1961 | Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961), *J. Abnormal and Social Psychology*. |
| H | Gerbner's cultivation theory, "mean world syndrome" | Gerbner and colleagues, Annenberg School, 1970s. |

| Final | Aza Raskin invented infinite scroll (2006), regrets it, "200,000 lifetimes a day" | His own rhetorical estimate, framed as a guess in the script. https://mastersofscale.com/video/he-invented-infinite-scroll-and-says-he-regrets-it/ |
