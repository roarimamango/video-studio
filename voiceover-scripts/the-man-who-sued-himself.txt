Gerald sued himself. And no matter what the judge decided, Gerald was going to lose.

He slipped on his own icy driveway. His insurance only paid if the homeowner hurt someone else. So Gerald, the victim, sued Gerald, the homeowner.

He couldn't afford two lawyers, so he played both sides.

"Your Honor, the defendant never salted the driveway."

"Objection! I was busy that morning."

"Busy doing what?"

Both Geralds: "Slipping."

He won. The insurance sent him a check for forty thousand dollars.

The next day, they raised his premium. By forty thousand dollars.

Three weeks in court, one broken wrist, and he ended up exactly where he started.

So he stepped outside, slipped on the same ice, and broke the other one.

He's suing the ice.
